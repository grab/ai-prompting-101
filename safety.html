Use AI safely

Five rules. Remember these.

AI is incredible. It's also new, uneven, and happy to lie to you with a straight face. These five rules cover most of the trouble.

01

Don't paste anything private.

No passwords, ID numbers, bank details, medical records, or other people's personal info. Treat AI like a stranger on public transport — if you wouldn't say it out loud, don't paste it.

02

AI makes stuff up — confidently.

Especially with numbers, quotes, dates, legal info, medical info, and very recent events. Always double-check anything that matters. A smooth answer isn't a correct one.

03

Check your course rules before submitting.

Some assignments allow AI, some don't. Use AI to learn and draft — not to hand in work as your own when your course says no. If in doubt, ask your tutor.

04

AI can reflect bias.

If an answer feels off — about gender, race, culture, class, or your own identity — it probably is. You're allowed to push back, ask for another angle, or just move on.

05

Some things need a human.

Real mental-health stuff, relationship crises, medical symptoms, legal issues — AI is a tool, not your therapist or your doctor. Use it to organise thoughts, then talk to a real person.

A fast gut-check

Before you paste something into AI, ask yourself:

  • • Would I say this out loud on the bus? → Safe to paste.
  • • Would this affect someone's grades, health, or money? → Double-check the output with a real source.
  • • Does my course let me use AI for this? → If unsure, ask before submitting.
  • • Is this a real crisis? → Talk to a real human first.
